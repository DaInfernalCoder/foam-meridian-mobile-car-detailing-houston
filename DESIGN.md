# Foam Meridian design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want the service scope to move clearly from first rinse through final review. The page should explain that path and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Meridian ink — `#132A32`
- Deep teal — `#1F4650`
- Foam aqua — `#36A8A0`
- Rinse white — `#F5FAF7`
- Marker coral — `#FF7A45`
- Display: Barlow Condensed
- Body: Manrope

## Layout thesis

The route frame becomes a meridian: one foam line crosses a central inspection axis through the real service stages.

## Signature

A dotted center meridian gives the moving foam line a clear before-and-after reference.

## Critique

The obvious option was bubbly decoration. The revision uses one controlled foam path so the identity stays technical and readable.
